{{% notice %}} ### Create a custom label to use to apply a policy to your device only
{{% /notice %}}

1. Click “Admin settings” in the left pane of the management console.

    ![case1_1](003_lab_use_cases_images/case1_1.png)

2. Click “Labels” and then “Create new custom label”
  
    ![case1_2](003_lab_use_cases_images/case1_2.png)

3. Enter your first initial last name in the “Name” text box (ex. John Smith will enter “jsmith”)

    ![case1_3](003_lab_use_cases_images/case1_3.png)
